OUR PURPOSE

About NeuroCare

A student prototype exploring multimodal Parkinson’s screening and progress tracking.

❋

SCREENING · TRACKING · AWARENESS

Technology can support a conversation.
It cannot replace a clinician.

NeuroCare brings together a spiral-image model and planned gait and voice assessment modules in one interface. The goal is to make assessment history easier to organise and understand, while being transparent about what the technology can and cannot do.
