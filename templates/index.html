OUR PURPOSE

About NeuroCare

A student prototype exploring multimodal Parkinson’s screening and progress tracking.

❋

SCREENING · TRACKING · AWARENESS

Technology can support a conversation.
It cannot replace a clinician.

NeuroCare brings together spiral image, sustained-vowel voice, and insole-sensor gait research models in one interface. The goal is to make assessment history easier to organise and understand, while being transparent about what the technology can and cannot do.

◎

Spiral analysis

Connects to the current image-based model in this project.

♙

Gait analysis

Research classifier trained on 165 labeled insole-sensor recordings; not validated for consumer phone sensors.

♫

Voice analysis

Research classifier evaluated on 81 labeled sustained-vowel recordings; score is not a calibrated probability.

ⓘ

For research and educational use
This application is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and should not be used to make treatment decisions.
